2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, mobile device or other electronic device when you visit a website[cite: 17].

They allow websites to recognise a visitor's device, remember certain preferences and collect information about how the website is being used[cite: 17].

Some cookies are necessary for a website to function correctly, while others assist with website analytics, performance monitoring, functionality and marketing activities[cite: 17].
