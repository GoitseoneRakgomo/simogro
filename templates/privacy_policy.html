1. Introduction

Simogro FZCO ("Simogro", "the Company", "we", "us" or "our") respects the privacy and confidentiality of individuals who visit our website, communicate with us or otherwise interact with our business.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, disclose and protect personal information obtained through our website and in the course of our business activities. It also explains the rights available to individuals in relation to their personal information and how those rights may be exercised.

We are committed to handling personal information responsibly, transparently and securely. We collect and process personal information only where there is a legitimate and appropriate business purpose for doing so.

By accessing or using our website, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Privacy Policy.

2. Company Information

Simogro FZCO is a company incorporated and registered in the United Arab Emirates and engaged in investment in commercial enterprises and management.

For purposes of this Privacy Policy, Simogro FZCO is responsible for determining how and why personal information collected through its website and business communications is processed.

3. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on the nature of your interaction with us, we may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, information generated through your use of our website and information received from third parties in connection with our business activities.

The personal information we may collect includes your name, surname, email address, telephone number, business address, company name, professional position and other contact information.

Where necessary for a business relationship, corporate engagement, investment or due diligence process, we may also collect identification information, nationality, country of residence, corporate affiliation, professional background, beneficial ownership information and other relevant business or verification information.

We may collect information relating to communications with us, including enquiries, correspondence, documents submitted to us and information provided through website contact forms.

We do not intentionally collect personal information that is unnecessary for the purpose for which it is required.

4. How We Collect Personal Information

We collect personal information directly from you when you complete a contact form, send us an email, submit documents, request information or otherwise communicate with us.

We may also receive personal information from companies, professional advisers, business associates, service providers, corporate representatives and other third parties where such information is relevant to an existing or proposed business relationship.

In addition, certain technical information may be collected automatically through cookies, server logs, analytics tools and similar technologies when you access our website.

Where personal information is obtained from a third party, we take reasonable steps to ensure that the information is relevant to the purpose for which it is being processed.

5. Purposes for Which We Use Personal Information

We may process personal information for the purposes of operating and maintaining our website, responding to enquiries, communicating with visitors, evaluating potential business opportunities and establishing or managing business relationships.

We may also use personal information to conduct corporate due diligence, verify the identity and background of relevant persons, assess business counterparties, evaluate investment opportunities and manage our internal corporate and investment activities.

Where appropriate, personal information may be processed to meet compliance, verification, recordkeeping, audit, risk management, security and administrative requirements.

We may further use technical information to monitor website performance, improve functionality, understand visitor engagement, identify technical problems and protect the website against unauthorised access or misuse.

We will not use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the purpose for which it was originally collected unless an appropriate justification exists.

6. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information where there is an appropriate and lawful basis for doing so.

This may include circumstances where you have provided your consent, where processing is necessary to respond to your request, where it is necessary to establish or perform a business relationship, where we have a legitimate business interest in carrying out the relevant processing or where processing is necessary to meet an applicable legal or regulatory obligation.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. The withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

7. Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

However, we may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary for the operation of our business and the fulfilment of legitimate business purposes.

Such disclosure may be made to our affiliated entities, subsidiaries, parent companies, business partners, professional advisers, auditors, consultants, banks, financial institutions, technology providers, website administrators and other service providers where their involvement is necessary for the relevant business purpose.

We may also disclose personal information to competent governmental, regulatory, judicial or law enforcement authorities where disclosure is required or properly authorised.

Where personal information is shared with third parties, we take reasonable steps to ensure that such parties are subject to appropriate confidentiality and data protection obligations.

8. International Transfers of Personal Information

Given the international nature of our corporate and investment activities, personal information may be accessed, processed or transferred outside the United Arab Emirates.

Such transfers may occur where we engage international professional advisers, technology providers, affiliated companies, business partners or other service providers.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect the information and maintain its confidentiality and security.

9. Retention of Personal Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, maintain our business relationships, comply with applicable recordkeeping requirements, resolve disputes and protect our legitimate business interests.

The retention period will depend on the nature of the information, the purpose of its collection and any applicable obligations relating to its preservation.

When personal information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete, destroy or anonymise it.

10. Security of Personal Information

We take reasonable technical, organisational and administrative measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

These measures may include access controls, password protection, secure information storage, restricted access to confidential records and the use of appropriate technological safeguards.

Although we take reasonable steps to protect personal information, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted through the internet.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable requirements and any legitimate grounds for retaining or processing information, you may request access to personal information we hold about you.

You may also request that inaccurate or incomplete personal information be corrected, or deleted where appropriate, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, object to certain processing activities or request that the processing of your personal information be restricted.

Where applicable, you may also request a copy of your personal information in a commonly used electronic format or request that it be transferred to another organisation.

We may request additional information to verify your identity before processing a privacy-related request. We will respond within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business activities, website functionality, information-handling practices or applicable requirements.

Any amendments will be published on this website together with an updated revision date.

Visitors are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically to remain informed about how their personal information is handled.
